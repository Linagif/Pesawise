# PesaWise User Guide

## Getting started
1. Open the site and click **Create an account** (full name, email, username, password). You are logged in automatically.
2. The dashboard starts empty. Follow the three steps: record income, create this month's budget, record a purchase.

## Record money you receive (Income)
**Income → Record income.** Pick the source (parents, casual jobs, freelancing, tutoring, small business, scholarship, other),
enter the amount, the date you actually received it and a short description. Only recorded income counts, so there is no need
to receive money on a fixed date. Add your own sources under **Categories**.
Use **Save and add another** to enter several payments. Filter by source or dates; the right-hand panel compares sources.

## Plan a monthly budget
**Budgets → New budget.** Choose the month and year (one budget per month). Tick "start from my most recent budget" to copy limits.
Open the budget and add a spending limit per category (e.g. Food 2,500). Adding the **Savings** line plans how much you
want to save that month. Progress bars show how much of each limit you have used:
green = on track, yellow = 80% or more, red = over the limit. You can still record spending when over budget; PesaWise just shows it.

## Record daily expenses (the most important habit)
Press **+ Expense** (top bar, any page) or **Record expense** on the dashboard. Enter amount, description and category;
the date is today unless you change it. Choose how you paid and whether it was planned. Press **Save expense**, or
**Save and add another** from the full form. After saving you see your new balance and any budget warning.
- **Daily Expenses** shows today (or any day with the arrows/date picker): total spent, number of purchases, highest expense,
  spending by category, week and month totals, and what is left of your daily target (set it under **Profile**).
- **All expenses** on that page lists everything with search and filters. Use the pencil to fix a mistake and the bin to delete (you confirm first).

## Savings goals
**Savings → New goal**: name, target, target date, and "already saved" if you had money set aside before using PesaWise
(it does not change your balance). Open a goal to **add savings**. Each contribution moves money out of your available balance
into the goal. It is not counted as an expense. The goal shows progress and the amount remaining.

## Dashboard, reports and tips
- **Dashboard:** six summary cards, today's summary, tips, five charts, budget progress and recent transactions (search and filter).
- **Reports:** daily, weekly, monthly, income, expense, savings and budget performance. Use **Print / save as PDF**.
- **Financial Tips:** simple suggestions based on your own records. They are prompts to review your habits, not professional financial advice.

## Account
**Profile** lets you change your name, email, daily spending target and password. **Logout** is at the bottom of the menu.

## Troubleshooting
| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "This looks like a duplicate" | You saved the same expense twice within seconds. If it is genuine, wait a few seconds and save again. |
| Balance is negative | You recorded more spending/savings than income. Check that all money received has been entered. |
| Cannot delete a category | It is used by records. Delete or edit those records first. |
| Forgot password | Use "Forgot password?" on the login page. |
